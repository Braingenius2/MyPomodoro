Definition

ICFR is about the numbers, not every control in the bank.

Internal control over financial reporting is the set of people, systems, and processes that gives reasonable assurance about the reliability of financial reporting and the preparation of financial statements for external use. In Nigeria those statements are prepared under IFRS.

In scope

What can misstate the financial statements

How loans, deposits, interest, fees, treasury positions, and manual journals are captured, calculated, interfaced, reviewed, and reported. Includes the information systems that do that work.

Out of scope unless it hits the numbers

Not a general IT audit

A firewall finding, a branch cash procedure, or a cyber incident matters here only if it can cause a material misstatement, a failed control over reporting, or a fraud involving someone with a significant reporting role.

Business event→Source system→Interface or batch→Calculation→General ledger→Report and review

Assertions you will hear, in bank language:

  • Completeness: every loan, deposit, fee, and deal that happened is in the number.
  • Existence or occurrence: what is in the number actually happened and the asset or liability is real.
  • Accuracy and valuation: interest, fees, fair value, and expected credit loss are calculated on the right rules.
  • Cutoff: the event is in the right period.
  • Presentation and disclosure: classification, notes, and regulatory versus IFRS differences are not hiding a misstatement.
  • Rights and obligations: the bank actually owns the asset or owes the liability.

Nigeria, at the level you can say out loud

Management owns the conclusion. A material weakness blocks it.

Public interest entities in Nigeria are expected to maintain internal control over financial reporting and to report on its effectiveness. The Financial Reporting Council cites section 7(2)(f) of the FRC Act 2011 as the statutory hook: management reports on internal control, including information systems controls, and an independent practitioner attests.

Listed entities

SEC guideline on ICFR

Listed companies, which includes listed banks, are directed to the SEC guideline rather than the FRC management-report guidance. The board is responsible for the integrity of financial controls and reporting. Do not quote the wrong document in a meeting.

Other public interest entities

FRC management guidance

The FRC guidance describes a top-down, risk-based evaluation and says a suitable framework such as COSO is used. It applies to public interest entities that are not in the SEC-listed bucket, with stated exceptions such as unit microfinance banks and insurance brokers.

One line that matters: if one or more material weaknesses exist, management cannot conclude that ICFR is effective. A material weakness is a deficiency severe enough that there is a reasonable possibility a material misstatement will not be prevented or detected on a timely basis. Your job is not to declare one. Your job is to understand what evidence would support, or fail to support, a control that protects a number.

Two other Nigerian facts, kept general:

  • Banks report under IFRS. CBN prudential rules sit beside that. A loan can look performing in a product system and still need a different classification or provision for regulatory or financial reporting. Learn which system produces which number. Do not guess a circular number.
  • CEO and CFO certification language in published bank annual reports typically covers design of controls, evaluation of effectiveness, significant deficiencies, and fraud involving people with a significant ICFR role. That is why access to posting, rate changes, and model changes is not an IT side topic.

Confirm the exact guideline, framework, and period the team is using. This page is not that guideline, and it is not firm methodology.

COSO, translated

Five components. You will mostly live in two of them.

Control environment

Tone, competence, authority, and whether people can actually override the process. Entity level. You note it. You rarely test it alone in week one.

Risk assessment

Which accounts and assertions can be materially wrong, including fraud risk. This is why loans and deposits usually outrank the stationery account.

Control activities

Approvals, reconciliations, automated edits, segregation of duties. This is where most analyst testing sits.

Information and communication

Systems, interfaces, reports, and whether the person reviewing a report can rely on it. Information systems controls live here and in control activities.

Monitoring

Internal audit, control self-review, exception trend review. A monitoring control can be in scope if management relies on it for the ICFR conclusion.

Sector difference

In a bank, the balance sheet is the product.

A manufacturer's ICFR worry is often inventory, revenue cutoff, and payables. A bank's worry is that the product systems are the books. Deposits are liabilities. Loans are assets. Interest is accrued by a job, not typed by a clerk. Fees are priced by a table. A failed interface is a financial reporting problem, not an IT ticket.

Volume makes sampling a weak default

Millions of transactions. If you are allowed a full population and the logic is clear, a reconciliation or exception query beats a sample of 25. That is why Python and SQL are engagement skills, not hobbies. You still need permission, an authoritative source, and a way to prove the query.

Two rulebooks, one customer

IFRS 9 expected credit loss and CBN prudential classification can both touch the same loan. Management may have a bridge between them. If you do not know which report feeds the financial statements and which feeds the regulator, you will test the wrong number.

Technology Risk on an ICFR cycle

You are there because the number is produced by a system.

A process auditor can describe the reconciliation. You should be able to say whether the reconciliation can be trusted. That usually means four questions.

ITGC

Can we rely on the automated piece for the period?

Access to programs and data, change management, computer operations, and program development. In a bank: who can post to the GL, who can change an interest parameter, who can migrate a model, did the interface batch finish, and was the failure reviewed.

ITAC

Does the system enforce the rule?

Limit checks, duplicate blocking, segregation the system will not let one user break, automated calculations. Test the configuration and the outcome, not a screenshot of a menu.

IPE

Is the report complete and accurate?

Information produced by the entity. If someone reviews a report, you care how the report is generated, what it includes and excludes, whether parameters can be changed, and whether you can tie it back to the source.

Interfaces

What happens to a record that does not cross?

Core banking to GL, channels to core, loan system to the provision engine. Completeness fails in the gap. Ask for the exception queue, not only the success log.

ITGC areas, in the words you will use:

  • Access: joiners, movers, leavers, privileged users, generic IDs, and whether a poster can approve their own journal.
  • Change: who can change calculation logic, rate tables, report definitions, and mappings, and whether that change was tested and approved.
  • Operations: batch completion, interface failures, backups only where they protect the reporting process, incident handling for jobs that feed the close.
  • Development: new products and new reports that create a new path into the financial statements.

Eight questions for kickoff

  1. Which processes and accounts are in scope this cycle, and which are explicitly out?
  2. Is this management's ICFR assessment support, external audit support, or something else? Which guideline applies?
  3. Which systems produce the in-scope numbers, and which reports does management actually rely on?
  4. Am I on ITGC, application controls, IPE, interfaces, or process support? Who reviews my work?
  5. What does a clean first deliverable look like, and where must the evidence live?
  6. Which tools and data environments are approved if a population test is needed?
  7. Where did last cycle's findings sit: access, change, reconciliation, or report logic?
  8. What would make my first week easier to review?
If the channel goes quiet: that is not permission to freelance. Skill work is the assignment until a person gives you one. When they do, stop the lab and confirm the definition of done before you open a file.
